== Description ==

The Usercentrics Privacy Policy Generator helps WordPress site owners comply with GDPR, CCPA, CPRA, COPPA, and other global privacy laws. Generate a tailored privacy policy instantly, available in multiple languages, and automatically updated when regulations change — ensuring your site stays legally compliant worldwide.

== Third-Party Service Usage ==

This plugin relies on Usercentrics Privacy Policy Generator, a third-party service, to retrieve and display privacy policy content.

**Service Provider:** Usercentrics GmbH (https://usercentrics.com/)

**What data is sent:**
* Your Policy ID (configured in plugin settings)
* Visitor's browser language preference (for multi-language support)

**When data is sent:**
* Every time a visitor loads your privacy policy page

**Why data is sent:**
* To fetch and display your customized privacy policy content

**Important:** No personal information, visitor data, or tracking information is transmitted to this service. The plugin only retrieves the policy content that you have created in your Usercentrics dashboard.

**How It Works:**

1. Install and activate the plugin
2. Create your privacy policy on [Usercentrics](https://policygenerator.usercentrics.eu/)
3. Copy your Policy ID from the Usercentrics dashboard
4. Go to Settings → PPG by Usercentrics
5. Paste your Policy ID and select which page should display the policy
6. Save settings - done!

Your privacy policy will now appear automatically on the selected page, always up-to-date with your latest changes on Usercentrics.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin files to `/wp-content/plugins/privacy-policy-usercentrics/`, or install via WordPress Plugin Directory
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' screen in WordPress
3. Follow the welcome screen instructions
4. Go to Settings → Usercentrics Privacy Policy Generator to configure

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Q1: Do I need a Usercentrics account? =

Yes. You'll create a free account on the Usercentrics Privacy Policy Generator platform to build your privacy policy and receive your unique Policy ID. It only takes a minute and ensures your policy stays automatically updated and compliant.

= Q2: Where do I find my Policy ID? =

After setting up your privacy policy in your Usercentrics dashboard, you'll find your Policy ID under your generated policy details. It looks like this: 2b3a23a0-94de-49c1-8bb2-0a4a847dd425. Copy and paste it into the plugin settings in WordPress.

= Q3: Can I customize the privacy policy text? =

Yes, customization happens directly in your Usercentrics dashboard. Any edits you make — such as company name, contact info, or policy sections — are instantly reflected on your WordPress site. You always stay in full control of your content.

= Q4: Does this plugin work with page builders? =

Absolutely. It's fully compatible with all major WordPress page builders, including Elementor, Divi, Gutenberg, Beaver Builder, and others. No extra configuration needed.

= Q5: Is this plugin GDPR compliant? =

Yes. The plugin displays a policy generated through the Usercentrics Privacy Policy Generator, which follows GDPR, CCPA, and global data protection standards. Final compliance depends on your specific settings and website practices.

= Q6: How is this different from other privacy policy plugins? =

Most plugins create a static policy you have to update manually. This plugin connects directly to Usercentrics — a professional consent and privacy platform — so your policy updates automatically whenever laws change. You never have to worry about outdated text.

= Q7: Do I need coding skills to use this? =

Not at all. Installation takes less than two minutes — install, paste your Policy ID, select a page, and publish. The plugin handles everything automatically in the background.

= Q8: Will this slow down my website? =

No. The plugin is lightweight (under 100KB) and built with performance in mind. It loads policy content asynchronously, so it won't affect your site's speed or Core Web Vitals.

= Q9: Does it work with my theme? =

Yes. It works seamlessly with all WordPress themes, from custom builds to popular frameworks, without requiring any code adjustments.

= Q10: Can I use this on multiple websites? =

Yes. You can install the plugin on as many WordPress sites as you manage. Each site will simply need its own Policy ID to ensure the correct privacy policy content displays.

= Q11: What languages are supported? =

Your privacy policy can be generated in multiple languages — English, German, Italian, and Dutch. The plugin automatically detects the visitor's browser language and displays the correct version when available.

= Q12: Do I need a Usercentrics CMP subscription? =

No. The Privacy Policy Generator works independently of the Usercentrics Consent Management Platform (CMP). You can use this plugin for free, whether or not you're using the CMP.

= Q13: How do I update my privacy policy? =

You don't need to update it manually. Whenever you make changes in your Usercentrics dashboard — or when laws change — the updates appear automatically on your WordPress site.

= Q14: Can I preview the policy before publishing? =

Yes. You can preview your privacy policy in WordPress before publishing it live. This ensures everything looks right and matches your site's style.

= Q15: What happens if I deactivate the plugin? =

Your WordPress page content remains untouched. If you deactivate the plugin, your site simply reverts to its original content — no data or settings are lost.

= Q16: Is my data secure? =

Yes. The plugin follows WordPress security best practices. It only communicates securely with Usercentrics servers to retrieve your policy text. No personal or user data is collected, stored, or shared.
